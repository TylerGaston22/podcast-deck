# Setup

Exact steps, in order. Everyone on the show is in one group. Each person either records their own show
(their PC has OBS) or just sends their camera. Nobody controls anyone else's OBS.

- **Part A:** the first person: start the group. Once.
- **Part B:** everyone else: join the group. Once each.
- **Part C:** every session.

---

## Part A: start the group

1. If you'll record: install **OBS Studio** from https://obsproject.com, open it once, then close it.
2. Go to https://tylergaston22.github.io/podcast-deck/ and click **Download for Windows**.
3. Open **PodcastDeck-Setup.exe**. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info** → **Run
   anyway**. It installs the deck, puts **Podcast Deck** on your desktop, and opens it. It updates itself
   from then on.
   (Developers: you can instead clone the repo and run **Install.bat**; that copy updates with git.)
4. Type your name, and click **Record my own show** (or **Camera only**).
5. Recording: type everyone else's names, separated by commas, and click **Start**. Then open **OBS**. The
   deck connects by itself, sets up all the layouts, and puts in a camera for everyone.
6. Click **Invite / Join** at the top right → **Copy link**, and send the link to everyone on the show.

To change a webcam or pick the browser window for Browser + Cams: **Settings…** in the deck.

---

## Part B: join the group

Open the group link you were sent. The page walks you through it:

1. **Download for Windows**, and open **PodcastDeck-Setup.exe** (More info → Run anyway, if Windows asks).
   Recording your own show too? Install **OBS Studio** and open it once.
2. **Join the show**: the deck opens with the group. Type your name **the way the others know you**, and
   pick **Record my own show** or **Camera only**.

If your name wasn't in the link yet, the deck copies an updated link for you: send it to everyone, so their
decks add your camera.

Someone new later? Anyone can click **Invite / Join** → **Add**, type their name, and send the updated
link (it's copied) to everyone.

---

## Part C: every session

1. Recording: open **OBS**.
2. Double-click **Podcast Deck** on your desktop.
3. Click **Start my camera**, and allow the camera and mic in the browser tab that opens. Keep it open
   while you're on. (Recording on your own, with no one else in the group, skips this: your webcam goes
   straight into OBS.)
4. Recording: click **Record**.

On the recording deck, drag any camera on the screen map to move it in your own show. Drag the small
square in its corner to resize it.
